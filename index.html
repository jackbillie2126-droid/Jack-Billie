What is ScoutTool?

ScoutTool is built for sales professionals, marketers, recruiters, and founders who need to discover public business email contacts quickly and safely. Simply paste target website domains, and ScoutTool will scan public contact pages to extract verified email addresses, source URLs, and company names.

How the ScoutTool Email Scraper & Finder Works

  1. Domain Discovery: Enter any list of company URLs or website domains into ScoutTool.
  2. Targeted Public Crawling: The scraper examines high-intent pages such as Contact, About, Team, and Support to discover public mailto: links and email patterns.
  3. Verification & Deduplication: ScoutTool validates syntax, removes duplicate entries, and links each discovered email to its source URL.
  4. Export & Outreach: Export leads immediately as CSV or TXT, or move them into the integrated email outreach composer.
